Terms of Service

Millie Manager is a private, single account tool run by Millie's family. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign up or use it.

The tool only uploads videos and captions that the account owner created and chose to share. It does not post to any other account, and it follows TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

The tool is provided as is, for personal use only. The owner can stop using it or remove its access at any time from TikTok's settings.

Privacy Policy

When the account owner connects TikTok, Millie Manager receives basic profile information (display name and open id) and permission to upload videos to that same account.

  • Data is used only to upload Millie's own videos and confirm they posted.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and are never shared or sold.
  • No data from other TikTok users is collected.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions. Revoking it ends all access, and stored tokens are then deleted.

Questions can be sent to Millie's family through the @milliebernard26 TikTok or Instagram account.
